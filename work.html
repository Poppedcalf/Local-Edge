VoltEdge Energy

Solar, backup power and electrical solutions. A fictional business.

The question it explores
A solar business competes on trust. How can a website help a homeowner understand residential, commercial and backup options, and feel confident enough to ask for a quote?
Design choices
  • Starts with the customer's problem, then moves to solutions, process and answers to common doubts.
  • No invented statistics, prices, certifications or testimonials. Illustrative content is labelled as such.
  • A short quote form, with WhatsApp as an alternative route.
  • A palette and typography deliberately different from Local Edge's, to suit the fictional brand.
Enquiry flow
Homepage, then the relevant solution, then process and FAQ, then a quote form (name, contact, location, need and message). In its demo mode, the form says plainly that nothing is sent.
Features
Responsive hero images, mobile menu, swipeable gallery, accessible FAQ, validated form with three submit modes. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

HospitalityConcept demonstration

Samanyanga Resort

Lakeside self-catering accommodation in Kariba. An independent concept, not a commissioned project.

The question it explores
How can an accommodation provider help a guest compare apartment sizes, understand what Kariba offers, and send a booking request, without a booking engine?
Design choices
  • A bedroom selector (two, three or four bedrooms) narrows the comparison before the guest reads detail.
  • A Kariba section explains the setting and activities, so the guest sees the destination as well as the room.
  • Facilities appear as short tags near the top, and common questions have their own FAQ.
  • The concept labels itself as a prototype and marks the nightly rates as placeholders.
Enquiry flow
Choose a size, view the apartment, complete a short booking form (apartment, dates, guests, name), then “Send on WhatsApp” opens a pre-filled message. There is no online payment.
Features
Bedroom selector, room cards, gallery section, FAQ, booking-request form that hands over to WhatsApp, and visible phone, email and WhatsApp contact options.

Outdoor & hireConcept demonstration

In Tents Outfitters

Mobile camp hire for hunting, fishing, safaris and retreats. An independent concept, not a commissioned project.

The question it explores
How can a camp-hire business make a complicated service easy to picture, and turn interest into a quote request for a specific place and date?
Design choices
  • Plain statements of what is included: beds with linen, a private en-suite, and full set-up and pack-up.
  • Three camp set-ups shown in a tabbed view, each with its own “ask for a quote” link.
  • Pricing “on request”, which suits a service priced by location and group size.
  • A short FAQ for the questions a first-time customer is likely to ask.
Enquiry flow
See the camps, tap “Ask for a quote”, complete a short form (trip type, dates, group size, camp location and preferred set-up), and “Send on WhatsApp” opens a pre-filled message.
Features
Tabbed camp views, captioned image gallery with a lightbox, FAQ, enquiry form that hands over to WhatsApp, and a WhatsApp button in the footer.

Local Edge (this website)

The studio's own site, built and revised in the open. Not a client project.

The question it explores
How does a new studio earn trust when it has no client history? By showing its thinking, publishing clear prices and limits, and being plain about what is and is not proven.
Design choices
  • Concept work is labelled as concept work, and there are no invented reviews, logos or results.
  • Packages, care plans, payment stages and exclusions are all stated on the page.
  • A published quality standard, an editorial Insights section and an honest build log.
Enquiry flow
Free-demo request or general project enquiry, each with a pre-filled WhatsApp fallback, plus a service-fit finder for an initial recommendation.
Features
One configuration file for prices and contact details, filterable portfolio, accessible forms, scroll-aware navigation. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on GitHub Pages.
