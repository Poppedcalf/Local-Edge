# Local Edge v7 — change log

Date: 3 November 2026

## Strategic repositioning
- Reframed Local Edge around customer acquisition and digital growth, rather than positioning the business exclusively as a website designer.
- Homepage now explains the customer journey from discovery and understanding to confidence and enquiry.
- Added four service pillars: market/customer research; digital presence/customer experience; lead-generation/outreach support; and content/campaign support.
- Preserved transparent website packages and care plans as specific delivery options within the broader offer.
- Expanded the contact enquiry choices to reflect the broader service mix.
- Updated main navigation and calls to action around starting a business conversation.

## Trust and scope safeguards
- No client results, testimonials, statistics or guarantees were invented.
- Research, outreach and campaign support are described as individually scoped engagements.
- Commission, attribution, fees, responsibilities and reporting are explicitly expected to be agreed in writing.
- Independent concept demonstrations remain identified as such.
