Prices are starting prices that reflect the intended base offer. The final price depends on the agreed deliverables, content, integrations and complexity.

03 Website care & ongoing improvements

Keep your website current, within clear limits

A website is not necessarily finished forever at launch. Business information changes, offers evolve, links need checking, and the online experience can be improved over time. These plans are optional, billed monthly and separate from the website price.

Website Care

$35/month

For a small business that mainly wants minor updates and routine checks.

  • Up to 30 minutes of small content or text edits per month
  • Monthly check of key links, contact pathways and agreed forms, where technically testable
  • Minor business-information updates within the included time
  • A short summary of work completed or any issues noticed
  • Standard support response within three business days, subject to the support terms below
Ask about Website Care

Website Growth

$75/month

For a business that wants regular updates and ongoing improvements.

Everything in Website Care, plus:

  • Up to 90 minutes of agreed content, image or minor layout updates each month
  • One prioritised improvement recommendation each month
  • A monthly check-in or concise written summary
  • Basic analytics review, when analytics are configured and you have supplied the necessary access and consent arrangements
Ask about Website Growth

Digital Partner

$150/month

For a business that wants a more active, continuing working relationship.

Everything in Website Growth, plus:

  • Up to three hours of agreed website edits and minor improvements per month
  • Monthly prioritisation of improvements based on your current needs
  • A review of agreed enquiry pathways and useful performance data, when data is available
  • Priority scheduling, subject to the response terms and existing workload
  • A monthly summary of changes, observations and recommended next steps
Ask about Digital Partner

The rules, in plain language

  • Monthly fees cover only the named plan and its included time.
  • Unused time expires at the end of the month, unless a written agreement says otherwise.
  • Additional work and major features are quoted separately before work begins.
  • A full redesign, new large sections, complex integrations, paid advertising and ongoing social-media management are not automatically included.
  • Domain registration, hosting fees, premium tools, paid plugins and other third-party costs are excluded unless the written agreement includes them.
  • Care plans are not bundled into website prices. They are a separate, optional service.

What “maintenance” means here

Local Edge builds lightweight static websites. There are no software or plugin updates to run, so care means small edits, checking links and contact paths, testing agreed forms and making agreed improvements. It does not include continuous monitoring or an uptime guarantee.

Billing, cancellation and support

  • Billing: monthly, paid in advance.
  • Cancellation: 30 days' notice, in writing (a WhatsApp or email message is fine).
  • Support: Website Care and Website Growth requests are answered within three business days. Digital Partner requests are scheduled ahead of those, subject to workload, and a specific response time is confirmed in your written agreement.

Why ongoing support can be more practical

Small changes, such as a new rate, a seasonal offer or a corrected phone number, are easy to postpone when each one needs its own quote. A monthly plan gives you a predictable cost, a known amount of time and someone who already understands your site. Existing clients can ask to add a plan at any time.

04 Scope, payment & terms

Everything important, written plainly

This is how projects normally work. Your written scope and quotation is what applies to your project, and this page is not legal advice.

Written scope first

Every project receives a written scope and quotation before work begins. Each package includes only its defined deliverables and stated revision rounds.

Payment stages

The standard structure is 60% to begin and 40% before final publication or handover, subject to written agreement. Timelines begin once the deposit, requirements and necessary content have been received.

Extra work

Additional pages, functionality, substantial changes and integrations may cost extra, and are quoted before work begins.

Domain, hosting and tools

Domain registration, hosting, premium tools and third-party subscriptions are excluded unless the quotation includes them. Where practical, you own the domain and your hosting or service accounts and pay those providers directly.

Hosting reality

The sites are static and can be hosted on GitHub Pages or another static host, agreed in the quotation. GitHub's published limits say Pages is not intended for running an online business or e-commerce site, so a site that mainly takes payments needs a different approach.

Your content

You provide accurate business information and images you are authorised to use, unless content creation or image sourcing is included in the quotation.

After launch

Maintenance and post-launch edits need a separate agreement or one of the care plans above.

Complex features

Online bookings, payments, live availability, databases and external systems need a separate feasibility assessment and quotation.

Ownership and handover

Final deliverables, ownership, handover details and any source-code arrangements are agreed in writing.

05 Pricing FAQ

Questions about price, revisions and scope

What do “from” prices mean?

They are starting prices in US dollars for the stated scope. The final price depends on the agreed deliverables, content, integrations and complexity, and is confirmed in a written quotation before you pay anything.

Will prices be discounted?

The base prices stay consistent with the package scope. If a time-limited introductory offer is genuinely available for your project, the quotation will show the standard price, the discount and the resulting price. It will never be presented as permanent or available to every visitor.

What happens if I need more than the included revisions?

Revisions are included within the agreed scope and number of rounds. Substantial changes beyond that scope may be quoted separately, and you will know the cost before the work starts.

Can I start with a website and add a care plan later?

Yes. Care plans are separate and optional, and an existing client can ask to add one at any time.

Can the site take bookings or payments?

The standard packages include WhatsApp and form-based enquiry and booking-request pathways. Live availability, reservation systems, online payments and databases need a separate feasibility assessment and quotation.

Is the free demo part of a package?

No. It is a limited concept preview, separate from the packages. Read how it works.
