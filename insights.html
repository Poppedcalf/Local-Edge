Facts Digital Facts & Insights

A few well-chosen facts, with their limits

Each fact shows its source, its date and what it does not tell us. Zimbabwe-specific data is kept separate from international research. Figures are reported as published, and none is an estimate by Local Edge.

Zimbabwe

Internet users

6.54 million 38.4% of the population

What it tells us: there is a sizeable online audience, and a large share of the population was not counted as online in this dataset.

Limits: the report says its latest figures “may under-represent current realities”. Not every internet user is a potential customer for every business. Social media is reported as 2.60 million “user identities” (15.3%), which the report says may not represent unique individuals.

Source: DataReportal, Digital 2026: Zimbabwe. Reflects the latest data available in October 2025 (end of 2025). Checked October 2026.

Zimbabwe

Cellular mobile connections

16.2 million 95.0% of the population

What it tells us: mobile phones are widespread, which supports designing for phones first.

Limits: connections are not people, and the report notes that some are voice and SMS only, with no internet access. It does not tell us how fast or affordable those connections are.

Source: DataReportal, Digital 2026: Zimbabwe. End of 2025. Checked October 2026.

International guidance

Google's recommended “good” page-experience targets

LCP 2.5 s · INP 200 ms · CLS 0.1 or better

What it tells us: a shared, published yardstick for how quickly a page shows content, responds to a tap and stays visually stable.

Limits: these are recommended targets measured at the 75th percentile of page loads, not guarantees. Meeting them does not guarantee more enquiries, bookings or sales.

Source: Google, web.dev: Web Vitals. Checked October 2026.

United States survey

Willingness to write a review

96% of 1,026 US adults were open to writing a business a review

What it tells us: in that sample, most people were open to reviewing a business when asked, which suggests that asking happy customers is worth trying.

Limits: this is a US adult panel, not a Zimbabwean survey, and it says nothing about local customer behaviour. “Open to” is not the same as “will”. The page gives a 29 January 2025 publication date but no fieldwork dates.

Source: BrightLocal, Local Consumer Review Survey 2025. Checked October 2026.

More facts will be added only when their exact claims, dates and sources have been checked. Correlation is never presented as causation, and figures are not used as decoration.
