FAQ

Questions you might have

How long does it take?

Most Starter sites are live in 3 to 5 days. Bigger packages take a little longer, and I'll give you a clear date before we start.

How much does it cost and can I pay in parts?

Websites start from $50 for the first 5 clients, then $80 for Starter, $150 for Business and $300 for Pro. You pay 50% to start and 50% when you approve the finished site.

Do I need to pay before seeing anything?

No. The homepage mockup is free. You only pay the deposit once you're happy with the look.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once the balance is paid, the website is yours.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, your logo if you have one, photos, contact details and a short description of what you do.

Can you update it later?

Yes. You can join the $10/month Care Plan, or ask for one-off updates when you need them.

Do you work outside Kariba?

Yes. I work with businesses all over Zimbabwe, by WhatsApp and phone call. In Kariba I can also meet in person.

What if I don't like it?

Revisions are included until you're happy, within the scope of your package.
