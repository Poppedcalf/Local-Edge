01 The boundary, stated plainly

What a free demo is, and what it is not

It is

  • An initial concept or preview of how your website could look and work
  • A way to judge my approach before spending anything
  • Free, with no obligation to buy

It is not

  • A completed, production-ready website
  • Unlimited revisions, a production handover or free ongoing changes
  • A promise that every request receives a bespoke full demo

A demo is limited in scope and subject to availability and suitability. A paid production project begins only after the final scope, price, terms and payment arrangements have been agreed in writing.

02 What to provide and what to expect

How a demo request works

  1. 1

    You send the request

    Use the short form below. It takes a few minutes on a phone.

  2. 2

    I review it

    I check whether a demo suits your business and my current availability, then reply by the method you chose.

  3. 3

    You see a concept

    If it is a fit, I prepare a limited preview, such as a homepage concept, and share it with you.

  4. 4

    You decide

    If you would like to go further, we agree scope, price and terms in writing first. If not, there is nothing to pay.

What helps me most

What your business does, who your customers are, the main problem you want to solve, and any website or social page you already have. A logo and a few photos help if you have them, but they are not needed to ask.

Demo questions

Will every request get a demo?

No. A demo depends on availability and on whether it is a good fit. If I cannot prepare one, I will say so plainly, and we can talk about other options.

What if I like the demo?

We agree the scope, price and terms in writing before any paid work begins. The standard payment structure is 60% to begin and 40% before final publication or handover. See services and pricing.

What if I do not?

Then there is nothing to pay and no hard feelings. The demo is free precisely so you can decide without pressure.

Can I use the demo as my live website?

No. A demo is a limited preview, not a production website, and it does not include a handover. A production site is a separate, agreed project.
